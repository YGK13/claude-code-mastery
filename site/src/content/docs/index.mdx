If you are a CHRO, COO, CMO, CFO, strategy or operations leader, you already run P&Ls, manage boards, lead change and build teams. That is most of the job. What you are missing is a specific, learnable 20%: real AI fluency, governance and ROI frameworks, proof you can ship, and a board-ready narrative. This program gives you exactly that.
