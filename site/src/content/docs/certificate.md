The CAIO Certificate is the self-paced version of the program for the executive who is not ready — or not available — for the live cohort. Same curriculum. Same frameworks. Same artifact rubrics. No live coaching.

It launches in 2026. Waitlist members get the founding price and first access.

## Who the certificate is for

The cohort is intense. Six weeks, weekly coaching, a board simulation. It is the right room for executives who are actively in motion — between roles, mid-pivot, or building toward a board seat in the next two quarters.

The certificate is for everyone else. The fractional CHRO who wants to add AI governance to her offer this year. The PE operating partner who needs to be conversant before the next portfolio review. The country head who is one promotion away from the AI mandate and wants the frameworks in his pocket before the conversation happens.

If you are 80% of a CAIO already and you want the missing 20% in a form you can work through on a Saturday morning, the certificate is for you.

## What is in it

All six pillars, in full. The cohort and the certificate share the same curriculum tree.

- **Pillar I — The mandate.** Why the role exists, what boards actually buy, the readiness diagnostic, your CAIO positioning map.
- **Pillar II — The landscape.** Vendor and capability mapping, the build/buy/partner decision, the AI stack as a non-engineer should read it.
- **Pillar III — Deployment.** RAG, agents, evals and the playbook for shipping the first internal AI assistant.
- **Pillar IV — Governance.** AI policy template, AI council charter, risk register, EU AI Act Article 4 literacy, NIST AI RMF mapping.
- **Pillar V — Value.** ROI modeling, the 12-month adoption roadmap, the cost-of-inaction story a CFO will believe.
- **Pillar VI — The CAIO operating model.** The 100-day plan, the board narrative, the org design, hiring the first three reports.

You also get every downloadable framework, every graded artifact rubric, a certificate of completion, and eligibility for a public portfolio page once your artifacts pass review.

## How it differs from the cohort

The certificate is roughly 75% of the value at a fraction of the price. Here is the honest delta.

| | Certificate | Cohort |

| Curriculum | All six pillars | All six pillars |
| Frameworks and rubrics | All | All |
| Pace | Self-paced | 6 weeks |
| Live coaching | — | Weekly, with Yuri |

What you give up in the certificate is the room — the weekly pressure, the cohort feedback, the simulation, the personal coaching. Some people need the room. Others ship better on their own. Pick honestly.
